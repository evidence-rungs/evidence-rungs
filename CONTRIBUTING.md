## Region and source cards

Start with `skills/evidence-rungs/references/regions/TEMPLATE.md`. Keep one short topic file per region. Every new or changed card must record:

- **Provenance:** canonical URL, exact hostname and redirects, original authoring statement, and where the source identity was checked.
- **Date:** source/effective/version date if known, checked-on date, and next review date. Unknown dates remain unknown.
- **Jurisdiction:** country/territory, applicable population or system, evidence language, and explicit exclusions. Sharing a language does not establish legal applicability.
- **Role:** what the source can establish, its independence/origin group, and one limitation or access condition. Approval, recommendation, measurement, and anecdote are different roles.
- **Reviewer and maintenance owner:** a consenting public handle or role, review status, and who will recheck or retire the card. Do not invent a reviewer or expose private contact information. An author alone is not independent expert review.

Submit **links and your own short descriptions only**. No scraped snippets, copied guidance, full text, screenshots, logos, PDFs, paywall material, forum archives, private examples, or provider outputs. A public URL is not permission to copy its contents. The inherited Austrian references are optional dated starting points with no independent reviewer recorded; they are not a reviewed global pack system.

Review should check source ownership/redirects, jurisdiction/version, role/independence, access/rights, and the usefulness of the warning. A link check alone is not legal or clinical review. Prioritize law/consumer/health at least every six months and fast-moving technology/community at least every three months. Mark stale cards explicitly or withdraw them; do not silently promise current law.

## Method and host changes

Explain the problem and smallest proposed edit. Use invented public examples, not real personal prompts. Add positive and negative checks for the touched rule, and test the actual host/version before changing compatibility status. A text test does not prove that a model obeys it. Keep public practice material separate from any private acceptance set.

No new provider, credential route, automatic installation, persistent profile reading, background work, telemetry, or agent-rule edits as a side effect. An improvement claim needs a prospective comparison with equal tools/budgets and rights-cleared evidence. v0.1 carries no performance numbers.

Use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for confidential issue reporting. Do not submit another person’s work or provider outputs under the project license.
