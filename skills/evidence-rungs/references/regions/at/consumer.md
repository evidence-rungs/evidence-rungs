# Austria: consumer rights (optional)
Reviewed 2026-10-19; review due 2027-04. Links and original roles only. Check jurisdiction, business location, and statutory basis before guidance. Affiliated organizations are not independent corroboration.

- https://www.arbeiterkammer.at/ — specialist: purchase/repair rights and practical guidance; an advocacy body, not a court or laboratory.
- https://vki.at/ — specialist: consumer enforcement, collective actions, and warnings; not guaranteed individual representation.
- https://konsument.at/ — independent-test role: consumer testing; many complete results require payment, never bypass it.
- https://europakonsument.at/ — specialist: cross-border consumer disputes in the documented European remit, not universal jurisdiction.
- https://www.verbraucherschlichtung.at/ — dispute-resolution role: recognized mediation for relevant Austrian businesses, not a binding judgment or universal participation duty.
