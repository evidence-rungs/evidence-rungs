# Austria: health evidence (optional)
Reviewed 2026-10-19; review due 2027-04. Links and original roles only. Match approval/product information to the regulator and recommendations to current guidelines/reviews. No diagnosis, individualized dose clearance, or forum-based safety conclusion.

- https://www.gesundheit.gv.at/ — primary: Austrian public patient information and service navigation, not examination or systematic review.
- https://www.basg.gv.at/medizinprodukte — primary: Austrian regulatory/device information; a conformity mark is not individual efficacy evidence.
- https://aspregister.basg.gv.at/ — primary: Austrian medicine product information, not personalized interaction or dose approval.
- https://www.cochrane.org/evidence — specialist: systematic reviews and evidence certainty; summaries may be open while full text is restricted.
- https://www.cochrane.de/ — specialist: German evidence explanations; not independent confirmation of the same review.
- https://www.awmf.org/leitlinien — specialist: German guideline entry point; verify class, validity, and Austrian applicability.
- https://register.awmf.org/ — specialist: guideline register and documents, not an Austrian reimbursement ruling.
- https://www.iqwig.de/ — specialist: German benefit/harms assessment; distinguish report from press summary and Austrian service rules.
- https://www.gesundheitsinformation.de/ — specialist: patient-facing evidence explanations, not remote diagnosis.
- https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance — link-only: identify dated guidance, do not fetch, quote, or analyze its content with an automated assistant; England/NHS scope is not Austrian care policy.
