# Scientific sources usable in Austria (optional)
Reviewed 2026-10-19; review due 2027-04. Links and original roles only. Indexes find papers; original studies/reviews support claims. Deduplicate by DOI or manuscript identifier. Metadata access does not license article text.

- https://www.crossref.org/ — aggregator: bibliographic and correction/license metadata; a DOI is not proof of quality.
- https://api.crossref.org/works — aggregator API: public DOI discovery; some abstracts have separate rights.
- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/ — aggregator: biomedical indexing and publication types; a record/abstract is not a full study.
- https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/esearch.fcgi — aggregator API: biomedical identifiers; anonymous pacing and content rights apply.
- https://openalex.org/ — aggregator: related literature/identifiers, not peer-review certification or complete field coverage.
- https://api.openalex.org/works — aggregator API: limited keyless discovery; linked papers retain separate rights.
- https://arxiv.org/ — primary: original preprint versions, not automatically reviewed consensus.
- https://export.arxiv.org/api/query — primary metadata API: one connection and one request per three seconds; manuscript licenses differ.
- https://www.semanticscholar.org/ — aggregator: discovery/citation graph; generated summaries do not prove paper claims.
- https://api.semanticscholar.org/ — optional aggregator API: anonymous availability is shared and rate-limited; a prior 429 is not a zero-result search.
